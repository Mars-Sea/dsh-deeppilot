DeepPilot 0.9.25

## 中文

- **会话中的提问与回答**。新版 iOS 客户端可在实时会话和历史记录中查看已提交的选项与补充文字，包括超时后的补答。
- **连接组件更新**。升级 WebSocket 依赖及远程连接使用的 Tailscale 辅助程序，并刷新六个平台的内置二进制文件。
- **兼容性**。现有 DSH 兼容范围保持不变，旧版手机客户端可以继续连接。

## English

- **Questions and answers in conversations** The updated iOS client can show submitted choices and free-text answers in live conversations and history, including answers sent after a timeout.
- **Connection component updates** The WebSocket dependency and the Tailscale helper used for remote connections are updated, with refreshed binaries for all six platforms.
- **Compatibility** The existing DSH compatibility range remains in place, and older mobile clients can still connect.
